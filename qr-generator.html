Best Practices for QR Code Design

To ensure your custom QR code scans perfectly every time, follow these simple rules:

  1. High Contrast: Always use a dark foreground on a light background.
  2. Quiet Zone: Keep a small empty border around the code so scanners can identify it.
  3. Test Before Printing: Always scan your code with both iOS and Android devices before finalizing your marketing materials.
