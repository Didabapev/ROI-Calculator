The Impact of Interest Rates and Loan Terms

Even a 0.5% change in your interest rate can result in tens of thousands of dollars in savings over the life of a 30-year loan. For example, on a $400,000 mortgage, reducing your rate from 7% to 6.5% could save you nearly $130 per month. Our interest rate estimator helps you visualize these long-term impacts, allowing you to negotiate better terms with your lender.

SEO Tip: Understanding the 28/36 Rule

Lenders often use the 28/36 rule to determine eligibility. Your mortgage payment should not exceed 28% of your gross monthly income, and your total debt payments shouldn't exceed 36%. Use our calculator to ensure your debt-to-income ratio (DTI) remains healthy before you apply for pre-approval.
