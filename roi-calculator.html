What is ROI?

ROI (Return on Investment) is a financial ratio used to calculate the benefit an investor will receive in relation to their investment cost. It is a key metric for startups, marketing, and real estate.

How to calculate ROI?

The formula is: ((Net Profit) / Cost of Investment) x 100. Our tool automates this for you instantly.
