Gross vs. Net Salary: What Is the Difference?

Gross Income is the total amount of money your employer pays you before any taxes or mandatory contributions are taken out. Net Pay (also known as take-home pay) is what remains after your local, state, or federal government takes its share. Depending on your location, these deductions can include:

  • Income Tax: The primary tax levied on your earnings.
  • Social Security & Pension: Mandatory contributions for future retirement.
  • Health Insurance: Deductions for public or private medical coverage.

The 50/30/20 Budgeting Rule

Once you calculate your monthly net salary, experts recommend the 50/30/20 rule: Allocate 50% of your income to Needs (rent, groceries), 30% to Wants (entertainment, hobbies), and 20% to Savings or Debt Repayment. Use our tool to find your exact numbers today.
